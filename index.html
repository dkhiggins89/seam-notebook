Seam Notebook

Sewing Planner for Android

Seam Notebook is an offline sewing organiser for your fabric stash, patterns, measurements and the machine settings that worked, from the first test seam to the finished make.

Get it on Google PlayFree to download

What it does

Machine settings that worked

Save fabric, needle, thread, stitch, tension and presser foot, with front and back seam photos.

Project planner and journal

Next steps, checklists, fitting notes and a progress journal for every make.

Fabric stash

Track what's left, fibre, width, washing notes and where it's stored.

Pattern library

Pattern details, the size you cut and your own PDF instructions in one place.

Gentle nudges

If a project sits untouched for a week, a reminder helps you pick it back up.

Offline and private

No account and no cloud. Your sewing notes stay on your phone.

Price

Free to download with ads. An optional in-app purchase removes ads.
